The Forbidden City / Palace Museum

UNESCO World Heritage • 600-Year-Old Imperial Palace of Ming and Qing Dynasties

The world's largest and best-preserved ancient wooden palace complex, boasting 980 surviving buildings with over 8,700 rooms. Served as the ceremonial and political center of Chinese government for almost 500 years.

5A级景区 / 世界文化遗产 3 Photos ¥45 (门票) + ¥140 (缆车往返)

Mutianyu Great Wall (慕田峪长城)

UNESCO World Heritage • Magnificently Restored Wall with Lush Mountain Vistas & Toboggan

One of the best-preserved sections of the Great Wall, surrounded by 96% forest coverage. Features 23 ancient watchtowers, cable car access, and an exhilarating mountain toboggan ride.

5A级景区 / 世界文化遗产 3 Photos ¥30 / 门票 (约 $4.5)

The Summer Palace (颐和园)

UNESCO World Heritage • China's Largest and Most Magnificent Royal Imperial Garden

Centering on Kunming Lake and Longevity Hill, this sprawling royal retreat combines classical pavilions, the 728-meter Long Corridor, and graceful Seventeen-Arch Bridge.

5A级景区 / 世界文化遗产 3 Photos ¥60
