Dujiangyan Irrigation System & Mount Qingcheng (都江堰与青城山)

2,270-Year Ancient Eco-Hydraulic Engineering Wonder & The Birthplace of Taoism

Built in 256 BC without a single dam and still irrigating the Chengdu Plain today. Qingcheng Mountain offers serene evergreen forests and ancient Taoist temples.

5A级景区 / 世界文化与自然双遗产 2 Photos ¥80 / 景区门票 (约 $11.0)

Leshan Giant Buddha (乐山大佛)

UNESCO World Heritage • World's Largest 71-Meter Stone-Carved Sitting Buddha

Carved out of a cliff face during the Tang Dynasty overlooking the confluence of three rivers. Walk down the Nine-Turn Plank Road or take a boat cruise for full panoramic views.

5A级景区 / 国际科普示范基地 3 Photos ¥55 / 约 $8

Chengdu Giant Panda Breeding Research Base (成都大熊猫繁育研究基地)

World's Foremost Giant Panda Conservation & Breeding Sanctuary

Spanning over 1,000 acres designed to mimic the natural habitat of wild giant pandas, offering travelers the chance to observe adult and newborn pandas feeding on bamboo in peaceful bamboo groves.
