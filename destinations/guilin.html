Li River Scenic Cruise (漓江风景名胜区)

UNESCO World Heritage • Emerald Waters & Mythical Karst Peak Landscapes Celebrated for Millennia

A picturesque 83-kilometer cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo resembling an unfurling traditional Chinese watercolor scroll, featuring Nine Horse Mural Hill and the famous 20-Yuan banknote scenery.

4A级景区 / 全球重要农业文化遗产 2 Photos ¥80 / 通票 (约 $11.0)

Longji Rice Terraces (龙脊梯田 • 龙胜七星伴月)

650-Year Mountain Farming Wonder & Ethnic Minority Villages (Zhuang & Yao Red Clothing)

Magnificent terraced rice fields sculpted layer upon layer from river valleys to mountain ridges. Splendid in green summer, golden autumn, and mirror-like spring.

4A级景区 / 国家级旅游度假区 2 Photos ¥160 - ¥320 / 筏 (双人筏, 约 $22 - $45)

Yulong River Bamboo Rafting (遇龙河竹筏漂流)

Peaceful "Little Li River" Hand-Punted Traditional Bamboo Rafts Through Karst Valleys

Enjoy serene countryside waters flanked by green bamboo groves, historic bridges like Dragon Bridge (Yulong Qiao), and traditional water dams.
