The Ultimate Guide to China's High-Speed Bullet Trains (Fuxing 350 km/h)

How to book tickets, navigate mega railway stations, choose seat classes, and glide between cities in total comfort.

By David Chen • Published 2026-08-01 • 7 min read

China boasts the world's most extensive high-speed railway network (over 45,000 km). Learn how taking bullet trains is often faster and far more relaxing than flying.

### Why China High-Speed Rail is the Best Way to Travel

Traveling at cruising speeds of up to **350 km/h (217 mph)**, China's Fuxing bullet trains connect Beijing to Shanghai (1,318 km) in just 4 hours and 18 minutes, city-center to city-center.

Seat Classes Explained - **Second Class**: 3+2 seating configuration, spacious legroom (more spacious than airline economy), power outlets, and free Wi-Fi. - **First Class**: 2+2 seating configuration, plush wide reclining seats with footrests. - **Business Class**: 2+1 or 1+1 luxury lie-flat pod seats, complimentary snack boxes, premium lounge access.
